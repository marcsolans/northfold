# Acceptable use policy

> **Draft — pending review by a lawyer.** This document is a working template and must be validated before final publication. The Spanish version prevails in case of discrepancy.

This policy forms part of the [Terms of service](terms.md). It applies to every organization, its users and the agents, workflows, interfaces and channels they build with Messer.

## 1. Prohibited uses

Messer may not be used to:

1. Break the law or infringe third-party rights, including intellectual property, privacy and image rights.
2. Generate, store or distribute illegal content, including child sexual abuse material, content inciting violence or hatred, or content promoting terrorism.
3. Send unsolicited commercial communications, spam or bulk messages without the required legal basis and consent.
4. Develop or distribute malware, carry out attacks, gain unauthorized access to systems or circumvent security measures.
5. Impersonate people or organizations, or make someone believe they are talking to a person when they are talking to an AI system.
6. Engage in AI practices prohibited by Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), including: subliminal or manipulative techniques causing harm, exploitation of the vulnerabilities of specific groups, social scoring, emotion inference in the workplace or in education except for medical or safety reasons, biometric categorization based on sensitive data, or building facial recognition databases through untargeted scraping of images.
7. Make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people (for example on employment, credit, insurance, housing or access to essential services) in a fully automated way, without human oversight or the safeguards required by law.
8. Monitor or track people without their knowledge or without a legal basis.
9. Breach the usage policies of the AI model providers used, including Anthropic's usage policy.

## 2. Responsible use of agents

Organizations must:

- configure human approval for actions with relevant external effects (payments, credit notes, bulk sends, changes in third-party systems); Messer enables it by default for tools with external effects;
- inform the people who interact with their interfaces and channels that they are communicating with an AI system; Messer's public chats say so by default;
- regularly review traces, evals and conversations to detect wrong answers or misuse;
- not include special categories of personal data without a legal basis and a prior assessment.

## 3. Service integrity

It is not permitted to:

- circumvent plan limits, budgets, usage limits or access controls;
- deliberately overload the service, including load testing without prior authorization;
- access or attempt to access other organizations' data;
- reverse engineer the service, except as permitted by law or by the license of the exported code and of the open-source software it is based on;
- resell access to the service without an agreement with Messer.

If you find a vulnerability, report it through the [contact page](/contact?topic=security) choosing the "Security and compliance" topic, without exploiting it or accessing other people's data.

## 4. Consequences of a breach

If we detect a use contrary to this policy we may, proportionately: ask for it to be corrected, disable a specific agent, channel or interface, temporarily suspend the organization or, in serious or repeated cases, terminate the service. Except in urgent cases or where required by law, we will notify you first and give you the opportunity to correct it.

## 5. Reports

If you believe someone is using Messer contrary to this policy, write to us through the [contact page](/contact?topic=other) with all the information you can provide.
