This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit our website, create an account or use Messer, what we use it for and what rights you have.

## 1. Controller

[Company name], with tax ID [●] and registered address at [●] ("**Messer**"). You can contact our data protection officer at [DPO email] or through the [contact page](/contact).

## 2. Two different roles

- **Messer as controller.** We process user account, billing, service usage, support and website data as controller. This policy covers those processing activities.
- **Messer as processor.** The data that customer organizations upload to Messer or that their agents, workflows, interfaces and channels generate (for example, documents, tables or conversations with their own customers) is processed on behalf of each organization, which is the controller. That processing is governed by the [Data processing agreement](dpa.md). If you are an end customer of one of those organizations, contact it to exercise your rights.

## 3. Which data we process and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, password (as a hash), organization, role, language | Create and manage your account and access | Performance of the contract |
| Billing data: company name, tax/VAT ID, address, last card digits | Charge subscriptions and issue invoices | Performance of the contract and legal obligation |
| Usage records: runs, credit consumption, audit, IP addresses | Provide the service, bill usage, security and fraud prevention | Performance of the contract and legitimate interest |
| Contact form and support messages | Answer your request | Legitimate interest or pre-contractual steps |
| Service emails (verification, trial notices, alerts) | Keep you informed about your account and organization | Performance of the contract |
| Product analytics (page views and events) | Understand and improve the product | Consent, only if you accept it in the cookie notice |
| Technical errors | Detect and fix failures | Legitimate interest |

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you.

## 4. How long we keep data

- Account data: while the account exists and, afterwards, for the limitation periods of any actions that may arise.
- Billing data: the period required by tax and commercial law (generally 6 years).
- Security and audit records: up to [●] months, unless needed for an ongoing investigation.
- Contact messages: up to 24 months from the last communication.
- Product analytics: up to 12 months.

## 5. Who we share data with

We use providers that process data on our behalf (hosting, database, payments, email, error monitoring and analytics). The full list, with their role and location, is on the [sub-processors](subprocessors.md) page. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

We may also disclose data to public authorities when a law requires us to.

## 6. International transfers

Our infrastructure runs in European Union regions. Some providers belong to groups headquartered outside the European Economic Area. When there is an international transfer, we rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for participating companies) or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, with supplementary measures where needed.

In standard residency mode, the content organizations send to AI models is processed by Anthropic. In strict EU residency mode, models are used only through AWS Bedrock in an EU region.

## 7. Your rights

You can exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to [DPO email] or through the [contact page](/contact). From the Messer settings you can also export your data or request its deletion.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

## 8. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk: encryption in transit and at rest, per-organization data isolation in the database, Argon2id passwords, two-factor authentication, an insert-only audit log and backups. See the details in the [trust center](/security).

## 9. Minors

Messer is a service for companies and professionals. It is not intended for people under 18.

## 10. Cookies

We use technical cookies required for the service to work and, only if you accept them, analytics cookies. See the [Cookie policy](cookies.md).

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will notify you by email or within the service before the changes take effect.
